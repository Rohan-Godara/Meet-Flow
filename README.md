# 🚀 Meet Flow AI

### AI-Powered Meeting & Follow-up Assistant

Meet Flow AI is an intelligent meeting lifecycle assistant that automates the complete meeting workflow — from scheduling and agenda generation to transcript analysis, action-item extraction, Notion synchronization, and follow-up emails.

Built for **Track 2 – AI Meeting & Follow-up Assistant**.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Meetings often involve multiple disconnected tools:

- Google Calendar for scheduling
- Zoom for video meetings
- Notes for recording decisions
- Notion for task management
- Gmail/Resend for follow-up communication

After a meeting, important decisions and tasks can easily be forgotten.

Meet Flow AI brings these workflows together into a single AI-powered platform.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

Meet Flow AI combines **Gemini AI** with **Swytchcode integrations** to automate the meeting lifecycle.
